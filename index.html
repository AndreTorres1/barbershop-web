Our Story

Craft, Character
& Community

The Sharp Cut was born from a simple belief: that a great barbershop is more than a place to get your hair cut. It's where you slow down, feel at ease, and leave feeling like the best version of yourself.

Since 2015, we've been part of Porto's fabric — a place where craft meets conversation, and where every client is treated like a regular, even on their first visit.
